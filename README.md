# Supply Chain & Manufacturing Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

This Power BI project analyzes supply chain and manufacturing performance using forecasting, inventory, logistics, supplier, and production data. The dashboard helps identify operational risks, cost inefficiencies, stockout issues, supplier delays, and production downtime.

The main objective is to support data-driven decision-making by converting raw operational data into clear KPIs, visual insights, and business recommendations.

## Business Problem

Manufacturing businesses often face challenges such as inaccurate demand forecasting, delayed suppliers, high logistics costs, stockout risks, and production downtime. These issues directly affect service levels, customer satisfaction, and operational cost.

This dashboard provides a structured view of these problems and helps prioritize corrective actions.

## Dashboard Focus Areas

### Forecasting Analysis
The dashboard evaluates demand forecasting accuracy using MAPE and RMSE. High forecast error highlights the need for better demand planning and exception handling.

### Inventory Risk Analysis
The dashboard identifies stockout-prone SKUs, low coverage products, and inventory imbalance across the network.

### Supplier Performance
Supplier delay probability, on-time delivery rate, and high-risk orders are analyzed to identify weak suppliers and improve procurement reliability.

### Logistics Cost Analysis
Shipment cost, transit delay, and high-cost shipments are tracked to reduce freight leakage and optimize transport modes.

### Production Downtime Analysis
Downtime hours, downtime events, delay days, and major downtime reasons are analyzed to improve manufacturing efficiency.

## Key Insights 

- Forecast accuracy is weak, with high MAPE and RMSE.
- 15 SKUs are at high stockout risk.
- 573 orders are classified as high-risk.
- Logistics cost is high, with $1.75M total shipment cost.
- Air freight appears expensive and should be controlled.
- Supplier performance is a major bottleneck.
- On-time delivery is only 27.38%.
- Production downtime is affecting output and replenishment.
- Inventory risk is concentrated in specific SKUs rather than across all products.
